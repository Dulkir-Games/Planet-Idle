# Design pillars

Working preferences for Planet Idle. Theme and specific mechanics are still open. this document captures how the game should *feel* and *behave*.

| Subject              | Intent                                                                                                                                                                          |
|----------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Genre**            | An idle game, intended to either be played actively or left open in the background.                                                                                             |
| **Systems**          | Combine physical motion, randomness, clear goals, meaningful decisions, and machines taking over work.                                                                          |
| **Math**             | Trade-offs are welcome. However, the hardest decision should need at most one or two simple relationships to reason about. If a choice needs a spreadsheet, it is out of scope. |
| **Tone**             | Visual satisfaction first. The game should stay interesting on a second monitor: simple rules, satisfying growth and motion.                                                    |
| **Input**            | Low frequency, high intent. Check in when convenient. There should always be something useful to do. No quick-time events or mechanics that punish being tabbed out.            |
| **Manual Actions**   | Less is more over time. For each new concept the player should do stuff manually, then unlock automations. Long-term play should skew toward decisions, not repeated chores.    |
| **Offline progress** | Optional later. Strong offline simulation is not a requirement for v0.1.                                                                                                        |
